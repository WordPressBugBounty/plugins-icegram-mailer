== Installation ==

= Option 1: Install Directly from Your WordPress Dashboard (Recommended) =

1. Log in to your WordPress Admin Dashboard and navigate to **Plugins > Add New**.
2. Search for **Icegram Mailer**.
3. Click **Install Now** next to *Icegram Mailer – Reliable Email Deliverability, No-Code SMTP Replacement & Email Logs for WordPress*, then click **Activate**.
4. Open **Icegram Mailer** in your WordPress admin menu, connect your free account in one click to activate your **200 free emails/month**, configure your sender details, and send a test email.

= Option 2: Manual ZIP Upload =

1. Download the `icegram-mailer.zip` archive from the [WordPress.org plugin page](https://wordpress.org/plugins/icegram-mailer/).
2. In your WordPress Admin Dashboard, go to **Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin**, select `icegram-mailer.zip`, click **Install Now**, and click **Activate Plugin**.
3. Follow the quick setup screen under **Icegram Mailer** to start sending emails immediately.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What is Icegram Mailer and how is it different from WP Mail SMTP or FluentSMTP? =

Traditional SMTP plugins like WP Mail SMTP, FluentSMTP, or Post SMTP require you to sign up for a separate third-party email provider (such as Amazon SES, SendGrid, or Mailgun) and configure API keys or SMTP ports manually. Icegram Mailer includes a **built-in email sending service** right inside the plugin — so you can fix WordPress email deliverability, track opens, and inspect email logs in minutes without managing external SMTP accounts.

= How many emails can I send on the Free Plan? =

The Free Plan includes **200 free emails every month** (resetting automatically each month in your site's local timezone), along with full access to Email Logs, Email Source Detection, Open Tracking, and One-Click Email Resending. As your email volume grows, you can upgrade to a higher monthly sending plan anytime.

= Are Email Logs, Open Tracking, and Resending Emails included for free? =

Yes. Unlike WP Mail SMTP and Easy WP SMTP (which lock email logging, open tracking, and resending behind paid Pro plans), Icegram Mailer includes real-time **Email Logs**, **Email Source Detection** (showing which plugin sent each message), **Open Tracking**, and **One-Click Resend** on every plan, including Free.

= Will Icegram Mailer override my Contact Form `Reply-To` addresses? =

No. Icegram Mailer respects `Reply-To` headers and includes a dedicated setting (added in v1.0.11) to customize your default `Reply-To` email address, ensuring that when you hit "Reply" on a Contact Form 7, WPForms, or Icegram Collect inquiry, your reply goes directly to the visitor.

= Is Icegram Mailer compatible with WooCommerce and newsletter plugins? =

Yes. Icegram Mailer works seamlessly with **WooCommerce**, **Easy Digital Downloads**, **[Icegram Express](https://wordpress.org/plugins/email-subscribers/)**, **MailPoet**, **FluentCRM**, **The Newsletter Plugin**, and any WordPress plugin that sends emails through `wp_mail()`.

= How do I know if an email fails to send or if I reach my monthly limit? =

Icegram Mailer automatically notifies the site administrator when an email fails to deliver or when your account approaches its monthly sending quota. You can also check your live usage counter, next monthly reset date, and delivery status at any time in the Icegram Mailer dashboard.

= Can I resend a failed or previously sent email? =

Yes. Navigate to **Icegram Mailer > Email Logs**, search or filter by status or source plugin, and click **Resend** next to any logged email to dispatch it again immediately.

= Will Icegram Mailer slow down my WordPress website? =

No. Icegram Mailer separates frontend and backend processing so your public pages stay fast and cache-friendly while outgoing emails are dispatched over Icegram's high-speed cloud API.
